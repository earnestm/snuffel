What happens on a walk

  1. 1Pick-upI collect your dog from home with your key or door code, on an agreed time window.
  2. 2The walkWe walk to the park, no van. Groups are matched by size and temperament, never more than four.
  3. 3Sniff timeDogs read the world through their nose. We slow down and let them, which settles them far better than a march.
  4. 4Home and updatePaws get wiped and the door locked behind us. You get a photo and route map with a quick note on how it went.

Where we walk

Pick-ups in Saint-Gilles, Forest and lower Ixelles. Walks go to Parc Duden, Parc de Forest and the quieter green corners in between.

Dogs stay on the lead except in designated dog zones, and only once I know they come back when called.

Just outside the area? Ask anyway. If it fits a route, it works.

Safety

Covered by professional liability insurance for animal care. A canine first-aid kit comes on every walk.

In heatwaves we walk early and shorter. In storms we reschedule and tell you first.

Every dog needs a microchip, up-to-date vaccinations and flea and tick treatment before the first walk.
